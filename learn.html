What this hub will include

Guides & explainers

Short primers on AI and work topics — written for non-specialists who need clear, evidence-backed context.

Teaching resources

Classroom-ready summaries, datasets, and discussion prompts drawn from FAIRWORK releases.

How to read our data

Walkthroughs of codebooks, limitations, and responsible interpretation of FAIRWORK findings.

Case collections

Curated examples across automation, job quality, surveillance, and policy — linked to primary sources.
