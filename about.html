About the Project

Understanding how AI is reshaping work and what we can do about it.

FAIRWORK is a research initiative at Purdue focused on workforce ethics, machine learning, and labor quality. We build open evidence for policymakers, researchers, and the public.

Mission & Vision

What we're trying to understand

The FAIRWORK Research Portal is a non-profit collective established to ensure that the AI revolution serves human prosperity. We bring together data scientists, sociologists, and legal scholars to provide an unbiased view of the shifting labor landscape.

Our vision

Help shape a future where technology amplifies human potential rather than replacing it. FAIRWORK investigates how AI is reshaping labor markets, job structures, and workforce policy - building structured databases from systematic review and qualitative coding of hundreds of academic studies.

GRAIL & Purdue

FAIRWORK is housed within the Governance and Responsible AI Lab (GRAIL) at Purdue University. GRAIL brings together interdisciplinary scholars studying the governance, ethics, and societal impacts of artificial intelligence - mapping trends in AI-driven automation, job displacement, skill shifts, worker well-being, and policy responses.

Core Research Themes
01
Automation

Tracking the pace of task displacement across 42 industry categories with focus on manufacturing.

02
Skill Transitions

Identifying effective reskilling pathways for workers transitioning into AI-collaborative roles.

03
Job Quality

Measuring psychological well-being and job satisfaction in algorithm-managed environments.

04
Human–AI Collaboration

Optimizing the interface between human intuition and machine predictive power in high-stakes fields.

05
Worker Surveillance

Evaluating the legal and ethical implications of continuous biometric and digital activity monitoring.

06
Policy Responses

Advising governments on legislative adaptive capacity to keep pace with technological change.
