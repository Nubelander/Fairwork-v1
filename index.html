How We Work & How to Use FAIRWORK
Rigorous, transparent,
replicable research.

FAIRWORK produces open evidence for policymakers, researchers, workers, and journalists. Every dataset ships with a codebook; every method is documented for replication.

Commitment to Transparency

We publish codebooks, inter-rater reliability scores, and full search protocols alongside every release so findings can be scrutinized and extended by others.

What our data can — and can't — tell you

Our evidence maps trends and associations in peer-reviewed research — not causal claims about every workplace. We flag contested findings and methodological limits explicitly.

Open Access Commitment

Public datasets, transparent methods, and freely available resources. Every dataset ships with a codebook; CC-BY licensed data available for researchers and educators.

Policymakers
Access evidence-based briefs on algorithmic governance, worker protections, and legislative adaptive capacity.
